TV box app or multi-device player?

Many IPTV apps are designed first for an Android box or TV. Kastideo aims for the same comfort, with one difference: it is free and also runs on Mac, Windows and Linux.

What differs

  • Devices. Apps designed for TV boxes are often limited to Android TV and similar devices. Kastideo runs on Mac, Windows, Linux, Android and Android TV: the same guide, categories and configuration from one device to another.
  • Price. Some apps reserve their advanced features for a paid offer. Kastideo is free, needs no account, and stays that way.
  • Maturity. Apps long established on TV boxes are very polished. Kastideo's Android and Android TV version is still a preview, while the Mac version is stable.

Which to choose

If you only watch on an Android TV and are happy, there is no rush. If you also want to watch on a computer, without paying, with the same organisation everywhere, Kastideo deserves a try. Your sources move between devices through a backup file: see Interfaces and personalisation.
