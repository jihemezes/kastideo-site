MultiView

Follow several matches, several news channels or an election night on a single screen: up to four live channels, with sound following the tile you pick.

How it works

MultiView shows one to four channels: one full frame, two side by side, three or four in a grid. Sound follows the active tile, outlined in orange. Click a tile, or press keys 1 to 4, to activate it.

  • The + button adds a channel from your favourites, recents or search.
  • On hover, you can change a tile's channel or remove it. The channel name and current programme are shown at the bottom.
  • Stop all cuts every stream.

You choose the number of screens (1 to 4) and the layout: side by side, stacked, grid, or one big picture with the others as small floating tiles. In the latter, you move the tiles, which snap to the nearest corner, and swap them with the big one by double-clicking. On Windows, Linux and Android, you can also save a group of channels under a name (for example “Football multiplex”) to reopen it in one click.

Several subscriptions or public lists

If you have several live sources, MultiView mixes them: two subscriptions, or a subscription and a list of public channels. Each source has its own number of connections, for example 1 for a limited subscription and 4 for a public list. Each screen is reserved for one source, and its channel picker only offers that source's channels. The picker follows the Classic interface: favourites, recents and categories on the left, channels with their current programme on the right.

Picture in picture

During live TV, the picture-in-picture button opens a second channel as a thumbnail in a corner of the player, without sound. A click on the thumbnail swaps it with the main channel. The second channel must come from a source with a free connection: with a single subscription limited to one connection, pick a channel from a public list or from a second subscription.

Why it is off by default

Each channel uses one connection to your provider. Since most subscriptions allow only one, MultiView stays disabled until you enter the number of simultaneous connections of your subscription, from 2 to 4, in the playback settings. For an Xtream source, the Test connection button shows this number.

Good to know

A recording or a download also uses one connection. If a tile shows “The channel is not responding”, the provider probably refuses one more connection: remove a channel.

Opening it

From the Playback menu, with a right-click on a channel (Add to MultiView), or with a keyboard shortcut. The channel you were watching becomes the first tile, and main playback stops to free its connection, unless it is being recorded.
