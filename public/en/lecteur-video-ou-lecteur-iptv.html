General video player or IPTV player?

A versatile video file player can usually open an M3U playlist. An IPTV player is built around television: here is what changes day to day.

The difference in one sentence

In a general-purpose player, an IPTV playlist is most often a long list of addresses to browse. In an IPTV player like Kastideo, it is a television: channels sorted by theme, a grid TV guide, replay, recordings, and your movies and series kept apart.

NeedGeneral video playerKastideo
Open an M3U playlistMost often yes, as a playlistYes, entries sorted into live, movies and series, with their categories
Xtream Codes subscriptionOften through the playlist address onlyCredentials, connection test, account status and the server's TV guide
Grid TV guideRarely2, 3 or 6 hour grid, themes, Tonight view, alerts
Replay of a past programmeRarelyYes, if your provider keeps an archive
Scheduled recordingRarelyImmediate or scheduled from the guide, with repeat (Windows, Linux, Android)
Movies and seriesFiles and addressesPosters, TMDB pages, seasons, episodes and resume playback
Several channels on screenOne window per instanceMultiView up to 4 channels, sound on the active tile

When to stay with a general-purpose player

A general-purpose player remains the right tool to play almost any video or audio file, convert a format or open an occasional network stream. Kastideo does not try to replace it: it is for those who watch IPTV television every day and want real comfort of use.
