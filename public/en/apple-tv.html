Kastideo for Apple TV

A version for Apple TV is in the works. It is being tested through TestFlight, Apple's service for trying an app before its release. We hope to see it on the App Store soon, with no date announced for now.

Not on the App Store yet

For now, the only way to try Kastideo on Apple TV is TestFlight. This version is a test: it may change and not all its features are described here.

Join the test

  1. Write to contact@kastideo.com with the email address of your Apple ID and your Apple TV model. You receive a TestFlight invitation.
  2. On the Apple TV, install the free TestFlight app from the App Store.
  3. Open the invitation, then install Kastideo from TestFlight.

Updates also arrive through TestFlight. Your feedback is valuable.

In the meantime

  • On iPhone and iPad, Kastideo is also being tested: see Kastideo for iPhone and iPad.
  • From a Mac, you can send the picture to an Apple TV with macOS screen mirroring: see Casting to a TV.

Kastideo provides no channels: content comes from your sources, whose legality you must check.
