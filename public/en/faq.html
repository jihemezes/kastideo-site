Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we are asked most often about Kastideo.

Does Kastideo provide channels or an IPTV subscription?

No. Kastideo is a player: it does not provide, host or sell any channel, movie or subscription. You bring your own sources and you are responsible for their legality. See Your sources.

Is Kastideo really free?

Yes. No feature is paid, no account is asked and no ads are shown. Support is possible and entirely optional: Support the project.

Which devices does it run on?

On Mac (stable version), and as a preview on Windows 10 and 11, Linux (Zorin OS, Ubuntu, Raspberry Pi) and Android, including Android TV. Versions for iPhone and iPad and for Apple TV are being tested through TestFlight, not on the App Store yet. See Download.

Which sources can it play?

An Xtream Codes subscription, an M3U playlist, folders on your drives or NAS, Jellyfin, Emby and Plex servers, and YouTube.

Why only one channel at a time?

Most IPTV subscriptions allow only one simultaneous connection. Kastideo closes the current stream before opening another to avoid exceeding your limit. With a multi-connection subscription, MultiView shows up to four channels.

How does replay work?

It relies on your provider's archive: when one exists, past programmes appear in the TV guide and play in one click. See Programme replay.

Why can't I see replay when my provider offers it?

Replay only appears if Kastideo detects an archive for the channel: the ↺ icon then shows next to it. Check in this order:

  • Replay is enabled: Settings > General > Replay.
  • The archive exists for this channel: some subscriptions keep channels for up to 14 days, but not all of them.
  • The guide contains past programmes: increase the number of days kept in the guide (Settings > General > Replay), then reload the guide. More days need more memory.
  • The time offset: adjust it if the programme that starts is not the one you chose.
  • The 6-hour delay: at launch, Kastideo only reloads a source if its last update is more than 6 hours old (adjustable). A recent guide or archive may therefore be missing: refresh the source (Settings > Sources, refresh button) to force a reload. By default, the guide also keeps only about 6 hours of past programmes.
  • A single connection: replay counts as one connection, close other playbacks and devices.

If the programme does not start, open the playback log, topic “Playback and relay”: it shows the archive address requested. See Programme replay.

Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, what should I do?

The Windows version is not signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. See Kastideo for Windows.

How do I install Kastideo on Android TV?

Outside Google Play, with the Downloader app, a USB stick or by sending it from a phone. See Install Kastideo on Android TV, or on Fire TV.

What happens to my password?

Your credentials stay on your device, in the system's secure store, and are sent only to the services you configure. Kastideo collects no usage statistics. See the privacy policy.

A channel does not start, what should I do?

First turn off any VPN, check that no other device uses your subscription, then look at the playback log. See Fast, stable and frugal.

How can I contact you?

By e-mail at contact@kastideo.com, or on GitHub for a problem or an idea.
