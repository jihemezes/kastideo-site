Kastideo for iPhone and iPad

A native app for iOS 17 or later. It is being tested through TestFlight, Apple's service for trying an app before its release. We hope to see it on the App Store soon, with no date announced for now.

Not on the App Store yet

For now, the only way to try Kastideo on iPhone and iPad is TestFlight. There is no file to install by hand.

Join the test

  1. Write to contact@kastideo.com with the email address of your Apple ID and your device (iPhone or iPad). You receive a TestFlight invitation.
  2. Install the free TestFlight app from the App Store.
  3. Open the invitation, then install Kastideo from TestFlight.

Updates also arrive through TestFlight. Your feedback is valuable: the playback log can be shared from Settings > About.

First launch

Read and accept the notice, then tap Add a source (Xtream Codes or M3U list). You can also import your configuration from a Mac, a PC or an Android device: sources, favourites and categories. Kastideo provides no channels: content comes from your sources, whose legality you must check. See Play an M3U playlist.

What the app offers

  • Home with resume playback, favourites and recents.
  • Live TV as a list, in Cinema display, by themes or in the TV guide, with replay when your provider offers it.
  • Movies and series in Cinema display or as a grid, with details pages, seasons and episodes.
  • Full-screen player, mini player, time-shifted live and sound that keeps playing with the screen locked.
  • Live recording, downloads, programme alerts.
  • My videos: Jellyfin, Emby, Plex, folders and the device gallery. YouTube.
  • MultiView with several channels at once, if your sources allow several connections.

iCloud sync

Optional and off by default, iCloud sync brings your sources, favourites, categories, display settings, resume positions and corrected pages to all your iPhones and iPads. Passwords go through iCloud Keychain, end-to-end encrypted.

What differs from the Mac

  • No picture in picture for now.
  • No scheduled recording: the app cannot wake itself on iPhone. Use programme alerts, or the Mac.
  • AirPlay sends sound to an Apple TV or a HomePod. For video, use iOS screen mirroring.
