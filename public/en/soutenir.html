Support the project

Kastideo is free, and stays free. If the app serves you well, you can support its development: it is entirely optional.

Buy me a coffee on Ko-fi

Really free

No feature is reserved for those who give, no account is asked and no ads are shown. A coffee is simply a thank-you, and time for the next versions.

Other ways to help

  • Report a problem on GitHub. Attach the playback log (copied in one click, credentials masked): it is what lets problems be fixed quickly. Windows, Linux and Android versions are previews, your feedback matters a lot.
  • Suggest an idea in the same place.
  • Tell someone looking for a frugal, fast player.

For anything else: contact@kastideo.com.
