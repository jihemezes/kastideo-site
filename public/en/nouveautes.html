  3. 1.146 November 2026Recordings (Mac): remove one, several or all recordings from the list, with or without deleting the files.
  4. 1.133 November 2026MultiView redesigned for several subscriptions: number of screens, layouts, a real PIP with movable tiles, new channel picker and more visible entry points.

How updates work

  • Mac: Kastideo quietly checks for updates at launch, then once a day. A banner announces it, never during viewing or recording. The update is optional and every update is signed.
  • Windows and Linux: the check happens at launch and then every 12 hours. Install and restart adapts to your installation type.
  • Android: install the new APK over the old one, your settings are kept.
