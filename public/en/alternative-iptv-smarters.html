IPTV Smarters Pro alternative

IPTV Smarters Pro is one of the most widespread apps for playing an IPTV subscription, often offered by providers under their own brand. Kastideo takes the opposite path: an independent player, tied to no provider.

What differs

  • Independence. Kastideo is tied to no provider, provides no channels and shows no subscription branding. You change provider without changing app.
  • Organisation. Three interfaces to choose from (Classic, Cinema, TV), a grid TV guide with themes, MultiView, replay built into the guide and queued downloads.
  • Your data. No account to create and no usage statistics: your credentials stay on your device, in the system's secure store. See the privacy policy.

Who it is for

If the app supplied by your provider suits you, you can keep it. If you prefer a neutral player, more complete and identical on computer and TV, try Kastideo with your own Xtream Codes credentials or M3U playlist: see Your sources.

Kastideo provides no content and you must check that your sources are legal. See the legal notice.
