Fast, stable and frugal

A player is judged by what happens when things go wrong. Here is what Kastideo does to start fast, last all evening and stay discreet.

Fast

  • Channel start usually in under a second, with a “Ready in x s” badge that measures it.
  • Adjustable buffer on Mac: Fast (default), Balanced or Stable, depending on whether you prefer immediacy or tolerance to interruptions.
  • An Xtream account loads quickly: catalogues of tens of thousands of items open without waiting, and get.php addresses are automatically converted to an Xtream source, which is faster.
  • Smooth navigation: moving from live to movies or series does not reload the catalogue.

Stable

  • Stream health at all times: a green, orange or red pill, with details on hover.
  • Start watchdog: if a channel does not start, Kastideo tries the other stream format (.ts or .m3u8), then shows an explicit error with the server's last answer, instead of spinning forever.
  • Frozen picture: when the picture stays stuck for more than 6 seconds while the stream keeps arriving, Kastideo reopens the channel with software decoding and remembers it. You can also enable it per channel or for everything.
  • Channel with no sound: Kastideo tries the other audio tracks and flags the problem with an icon.
  • One connection at a time: the previous stream is closed cleanly before each new one, so you do not exceed your limit.

Understanding a problem

The playback log keeps the detail of every opening: address tried, server answer, attempts, Kastideo's decisions. Credentials are masked in it. It is sorted by topic, filterable, searchable and copyable in one click, which lets you report a problem precisely.

First reflex

An active VPN often disturbs IPTV providers. Turn it off before digging further.

Frugal

  • No account to create with Kastideo. The app works with your sources, and that is all.
  • Your credentials stay on your device, in the system's secure store, and are sent only to the services you configure.
  • No usage statistics are collected by the app.
  • Discreet updates: the check happens at launch, never during viewing or recording, and installing is optional.

For the details of data, see the privacy policy.
