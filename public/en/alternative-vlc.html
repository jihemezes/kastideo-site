VLC alternative for watching IPTV

VLC is an excellent video file player, and it can open an M3U playlist. Kastideo is built around IPTV television: here is what changes day to day.

The difference in one sentence

With VLC, an IPTV playlist is a long list of addresses to browse. With Kastideo, it is a television: channels sorted by theme, a grid TV guide, replay, recordings, and your movies and series kept apart.

When to stay with VLC

VLC remains the right tool to play almost any video or audio file, convert a format or open a one-off network stream. Kastideo does not try to replace it: it is for people who watch IPTV television every day and want real comfort of use.

Try Kastideo

Kastideo provides no channels: you keep your sources and add them in the settings. See Your sources, the TV guide and replay, or download the app.
