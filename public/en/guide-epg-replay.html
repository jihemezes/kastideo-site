Set up the TV guide and watch a past programme

A complete TV guide, the ability to go back in time and watch something that already aired (replay, or catch-up): here is how to set it all up in Kastideo.

1. A guide for every channel

Each source can have a guide address in XMLTV format (.xml or .xml.gz). If you leave it empty, Kastideo uses the one from the Xtream Codes server or the one announced in the M3U playlist. The guide covers 6 hours back to 48 hours ahead and reloads by itself.

  • To change the address: Settings > Sources, the pencil button of the source, TV guide (XMLTV) field.
  • To restart the download: the Reload TV guide button (calendar icon). Under each source, Kastideo shows how many programmes the provider sent.

2. The backup guide

A provider's guide is sometimes incomplete. Kastideo can fill the gaps with a free guide from xmltvfr.fr: only channels without a programme are completed, the provider's guide keeps priority, and channels are matched by name.

Settings > General > Backup TV guide: turn it on or off, pick another guide, enter an XMLTV address, Refresh now.

3. Reading the grid

  • Channels are rows, hours are columns, and a red line marks the current time.
  • Now goes back to the current time, the date menu jumps to another day, and the displayed span can be set to 2, 3 or 6 hours.
  • Filter by favourites, recents, category or theme, or search for a channel.
  • Click a programme to open its details: Watch, Record or Schedule recording.

4. Watch a past programme (replay, catch-up)

Some subscriptions keep channels for up to 14 days, depending on the provider and the channel. Kastideo detects it by itself: the channels concerned carry the ↺ icon in lists and in the guide.

  1. Open the TV guide and choose a past day in the date menu (Watch again section), or click a programme marked ↺ directly.
  2. Click Watch. On the current programme, Watch from the start plays it from its beginning.
  3. During playback, the player bar allows pausing and seeking. At the end, Kastideo goes back to the live channel (this can be turned off).

Replay counts as one connection: it replaces the current channel, like a channel change. It depends entirely on your subscription.

5. Replay settings

Settings > General > Replay: turn it on or off, how many days of past programmes are kept in the guide (more days need more memory), time offset and return to live.

If it does not work

  • The guide is empty: check the address in the source settings, reload the guide, or turn on the backup guide.
  • The programme is shifted in time: fix the time offset in the replay settings.
  • A replay does not start: reload the guide after raising the number of days kept, then open the Log (topic “Playback and relay”).

See also: TV guide and themes, Replay.
