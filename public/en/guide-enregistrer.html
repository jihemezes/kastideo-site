Record a programme

Record live TV, schedule an upcoming programme, download a film overnight: everything is found in the Recordings section.

1. Record now

  • While a channel is playing, click the round red button in the player, or use the shortcut (⇧⌘R on Mac, R on Windows and Linux).
  • Alternatively: right-click a channel > Record now.
  • The recording uses the same connection as playback: nothing extra is used on your subscription.

On Mac, recording starts immediately, then a banner lets you choose when to stop: in 15 minutes, 1 hour, at a precise time or never. Without a choice, it stops at the end of the programme according to the TV guide (margin included), otherwise after the default duration set in the settings.

2. Schedule a recording

  • In the TV guide, open an upcoming programme then Schedule recording. A red dot marks it in the grid.
  • Right-click a channel > Schedule a recording…: pick a guide programme, or free times for a channel without a guide.
  • Margins before and after (adjustable) keep you from missing the start or the end.
  • On Windows and Linux, repeat is possible: every day, Monday to Friday or every week.
Important

Kastideo must stay open and the computer on at the scheduled time. On Windows, you can turn on “Launch Kastideo at sign-in” in the settings.

3. Download a film or a series

Right-click a film or an episode > Download or Download tonight. For a series, the details button offers the displayed season or the whole series. Downloads run one after another and can be paused, resumed or cancelled in the Recordings section.

4. A single connection: who goes first?

Your subscription often allows only one connection. Kastideo always asks before interrupting something: changing channel during a recording offers to stop it, and starting playback during a download pauses it until playback ends.

5. Find your files

  • Recordings section of the sidebar (⌘6 on Mac): in progress, scheduled, downloads and finished files. A click plays a file, a right-click shows it in the folder or deletes it.
  • Default folder: Movies/Kastideo on Mac, Videos/Kastideo on Windows. Changeable in Settings > Recordings.
  • At the end, the recording is losslessly converted to MP4 (Mac) or MKV (Windows and Linux) so it plays everywhere.
  • Expect roughly 1 to 3 GB per hour depending on the channel.

On Mac, you can also clean the list: remove one recording, a selection or everything, with or without deleting the files, and an Undo button stays for a few seconds.

Copy rights

Rights depend on your provider's terms: Kastideo records, for your private use, what your subscription already gives you.
